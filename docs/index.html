30 Quant Research Projects

Thirty questions in quantitative finance, from Monte Carlo and portfolio construction to stochastic volatility, statistical arbitrage, market making and deep hedging. Each one is answered out of sample, against honest baselines, with reproducible code and a section on what does not work.

A question, not a tutorialEvery project states a falsifiable question and answers it with a number.
Out of sample onlyRolling or walk-forward estimation, no look-ahead, fixed seeds.
Proper statisticsFormal tests, effect sizes and the textbook baseline in every comparison.
One command to reproduceEvery table and figure rebuilds from the repository.
