Honest Market Terminal

The usual fourteen numbers for each ticker, each labelled with what it actually forecast out of sample between 2012 and 2026. Numbers that carry no information are struck through.

Audit and method: the working paper. Daily data to the close shown; next-month means the next 21 trading days. Volatility forecast = OLS of log forward volatility on log 21-day realised vol and Bollinger %b, fitted on the asset's full history; range = 5th–95th percentile of its residuals. Research, not investment advice.
